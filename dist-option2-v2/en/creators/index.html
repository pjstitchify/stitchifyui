FAQ

Before you look.

Who takes part? +

Influencers who wear the clothes and share them are the center. Photography and modeling are separate sections on this page. The role is written on each brief.

Do I keep the clothes? +

A gift does not come back. A rental is returned by day 7 from receipt, and the brand pays shipping both ways. Both are written on the brief before you apply.

When does contact start? +

Before payment, contact details and DMs stay closed. They open after agreement and the brand's payment.

Can I apply with few followers? +

There is no follower minimum. Conditions differ by role and by brief. Read the conditions written on the brief.

Does opening a brief start the work? +

No. The work starts after both sides agree to the terms. Finding work, and the fee, are not guaranteed.

Is login required to review a brief? +

Yes. Reviewing briefs and their terms requires login. This page does not take applications.

What if I want to make clothes? +

Making clothes is MAKE, for brands. Photography and PR use a separate entrance.
