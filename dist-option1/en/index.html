FAQ

Before you proceed.

How is a specification created? +

Choose item, material, decoration and quantity, and export them as a production specification. You do not prepare a separate form.

What is the minimum quantity? +

Production starts at one piece. The specification and price depend on the item, decoration and quantity.

Which items and treatments are covered? +

Printing, embroidery and labels on blank bodies. Pattern-making and special specifications are reviewed, then accepted or declined.

When is the price confirmed? +

It is quoted from the item, decoration and quantity. Standard specifications can be checked on screen. Anything needing a separate review is quoted after that review. You see the price before you order.

When is an account required? +

Create your specification first, then register to save it and receive a quote. Begin by choosing the item, material, decoration and quantity.

When is the lead time confirmed? +

It depends on the item, decoration, quantity and production schedule. Lead time is given with the quote.

What does Showroom require? +

Apply with a brand account. It is separate from MAKE production, and you use it to commission a shoot or promotion. Fees are set for each request.

Are sales included? +

Sales stay on the brand's own channels. Stitchify handles production, and Showroom takes commissions for shoots and promotion. Sales volume is not guaranteed.
